A Few Times a Year

Winemaker dinners.

A few times a year, the kitchen builds a five-course dinner around a single winemaker, each course paired pour by pour. Here is how the last one went.

A recent dinner

G.D. Vajra & Luigi Baudana, Piemonte.

  1. Welcome

    N.S. della Neve Rosé NV

    A glass to open the night.

  2. First

    Riesling “Pétracine”

    Seared Alaskan halibut and diver scallop, coconut-lime reduction.

  3. Second

    Barolo “Albe”

    Peach burrata salad, prosciutto, balsamic.

  4. Third

    Barbera d’Alba “Superiore”

    Truffle-butter bucatini, alliums, pecorino.

  5. Fourth

    Dolcetto d’Alba

    Braised lamb shank, beet-root gnocchi, sage carrot purée.

  6. Fifth

    Moscato d’Asti

    Saffron vanilla semifreddo, strawberry jam.
