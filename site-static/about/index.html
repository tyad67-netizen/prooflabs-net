La Famiglia

Meet the Family

Macaluso's is a family restaurant in the truest sense. Laura and John run it together, with their son Max coming up behind them. We cook the way the family always has, pour the wine by hand, and treat the people across the table like our own. Pull up a chair, that's really all there is to it.

LM
Owner

Laura Macaluso

Laura runs the room, and runs the show. She is the heart of Macaluso's, the one who knows the regulars by name, keeps the night moving, and makes sure every table feels looked after.

JM
Owner

John Macaluso

John runs Macaluso's alongside Laura, a steady hand at the front of the house. He'll meet you at the door and see to it that you leave full and well taken care of.

MM
Next Generation

Max Macaluso

Max, Laura and John's son, is the next chapter of Macaluso's. He grew up in these rooms and is learning the business end to end, ready to carry the family name forward.

JG
Head Chef

Joseph "Rose" Greene

Joseph "Rose" Greene runs the kitchen, cooking the family's recipes from scratch every night, from the fresh pasta to the slow sauces. (Photo to come.)

SM
Sommelier

Scott Murphy

Scott keeps the wine list, every bottle chosen by hand. An Old World eye, a deep cellar, and a knack for the right pour for the night.
