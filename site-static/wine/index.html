The Sommelier

Every bottle, by hand.

Scott Murphy is our sommelier. He chooses every bottle on the list by hand, with the kind of knowledge that takes a lifetime to earn.

He looks for wines that belong on an Italian table, bottles made for red sauce and a long dinner, and a few that catch you off guard. When he finds one he loves, he brings in a little and keeps it on the list until it's gone.

Old World roots, a sharp eye, and a list that quietly gets better every season. Buon vino fa buon sangue.

Now and then

Winemaker dinners.

A few times a year, Macaluso's builds a dinner around a single producer: five courses, one estate, one night. The last one poured the wines of G.D. Vajra, from Barolo. Call to hear when the next one lands.

  1. Welcome

    N.S. della Neve Rosé NV

    A glass to open the night.

  2. First

    Riesling “Pétracine”

    Seared Alaskan halibut and diver scallop, coconut-lime reduction.

  3. Second

    Barolo “Albe”

    Peach burrata salad, prosciutto, balsamic.

  4. Third

    Barbera d'Alba “Superiore”

    Truffle-butter bucatini, alliums, pecorino.

  5. Fourth

    Dolcetto d'Alba

    Braised lamb shank, beet-root gnocchi, sage carrot purée.

  6. Fifth

    Moscato d'Asti

    Saffron vanilla semifreddo, strawberry jam.
